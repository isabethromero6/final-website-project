Peru

Machu Picchu is an amazing ancient city built by the Inca people high up in the Andes Mountains of Peru. Around 1450 AD, an emperor named Pachacuti ordered the secret royal mountain retreat built. Today, it is famous as one of the new Seven Wonders of the World.

Why Visit Peru?

Peru is one of the most beautiful countries in South America. This website teaches visitors about Peru's culture, food, history, and famous places.

Fun Facts

  • Peru has more than 4,000 varieties of potatoes.
  • Machu Picchu was built by the Incas around 1450.
  • About 60% of Peru is covered by the Amazon Rainforest.
